# D8-A Candidate-Threshold Coincidence Adjudication

## Confirmed gap

The ordinary policy-map \(C^2\) theorem does not cover candidate-threshold
coincidence.

The locked common-margin numerical design has

\[
q_0=q_1=q_2
\]

within every cell. Winner boundaries can therefore intersect moving
candidate-threshold boundaries exactly at the expansion point.

An explicit independent-Gaussian winner cell has unequal one-sided mixed
derivatives, so ordinary twice continuous differentiability fails.

## Consequences

- `d8a-theory-complete-v1` remains an immutable historical checkpoint.
- Its claim of completeness is superseded for designs with candidate
  threshold coincidence.
- `d8a-numerical-design-lock-v1` remains an immutable prospective design
  record.
- Scientific execution under that design is blocked pending theory repair.
- No completed D8-A scientific simulation is affected.

## Valid part retained

The following remain valid:

- scalar and joint empirical-quantile results;
- exact finite-\(n\) covariance identity;
- ordinary \(C^2\) policy theorem away from all relevant boundary
  coincidences;
- the smooth-region second-order bias formula.

piecewise quadratic expansion over polyhedral threshold-order cones.
