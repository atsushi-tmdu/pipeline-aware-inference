# D8-A Scalar Quantile Proof Adjudication

## Established

Lemma D8-A.2a proves the scalar empirical-quantile mean expansion under the
stated local smoothness and tail-integrability assumptions.

The proof explicitly retains:

- the exact order statistic \(k_B=\lceil Bp\rceil\);
- the lattice offset \(a_{p,B}\);
- the density-derivative curvature term;
- the possibility that the second-order coefficient depends on \(B\).

## Not established by this lemma

- the joint vector expansion;
- candidate-trigger covariance beyond first order;
- a uniform multivariate remainder;
- the required joint \(2+\eta\) moment bound;
- the policy-map \(C^2\) theorem;
- the final D8-A bias theorem;
- the TESS corollary.

## Status change

The scalar component of the joint quantile lemma is now treated as proved
under explicit assumptions. The full Lemma D8-A.2 remains incomplete.
