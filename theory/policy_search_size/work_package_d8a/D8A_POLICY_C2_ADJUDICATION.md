# D8-A Policy \(C^2\) Theorem Adjudication

## Established

Under fixed finite pools, continuous unique winners, strict threshold
separation, and explicit coordinate trace regularity:

- the winner-cell policy probabilities are \(C^2\);
- the policy contrast \(\Delta_\pi(\theta)\) is \(C^2\);
- its gradient agrees with the D7 boundary coefficients;
- its Hessian retains candidate-candidate, candidate-trigger, and
  trigger-trigger terms.

## Strength of assumptions

The coordinate trace assumptions are stronger and more explicit than merely
requiring a twice differentiable joint density. They are adopted to make the
moving-boundary proof complete without hiding boundary domination or
codimension-two conditions.
