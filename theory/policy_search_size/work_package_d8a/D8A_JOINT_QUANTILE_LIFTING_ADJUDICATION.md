# D8-A Joint Quantile Lifting Adjudication

## Established

- The scalar \(B^{-1}\) mean expansion is proved under the assumptions of
  Lemma D8-A.2a.
- The joint first-order covariance formula is proved conditional on a joint
  \(L^2\) Bahadur remainder.
- The fixed-dimensional vector \(2+\eta\) moment bound reduces to scalar
  component bounds.

## Still open

- proving the \(L^2\) Bahadur remainder from primitive density assumptions;
- proving the scalar \(2+\eta\) moment bound under the final tail conditions;
- combining these ingredients into an unconditional full joint quantile
  lemma;
- the general policy-map \(C^2\) theorem;
- the final D8-A policy-bias theorem.

## Terminology

The joint lifting result is a proved conditional lemma. The full joint
second-order quantile lemma remains incomplete.
